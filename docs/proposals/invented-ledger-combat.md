# INVENTED LEDGER — W4-B, combat

Per D-018 and D-021: every choice below is authored glue tagged `INVENTED`. It fits the theme and the canon, contradicts no CANON or A row or the notes, and the designer may veto it. A veto is appended to DECISIONS.md and supersedes only that line. No CANON row was changed. All maths is integer (D-022).

The **objects** are mostly attested (`A` in `arms.csv` and `items.csv`: the Lagash phalanx's spears and great shields on the Stele of the Vultures, the Standard of Ur's axes, javelins and studded cloaks, the copper helmets of the Royal Cemetery of Ur, Naram-Sin's composite bow, the Hamoukar sling bullets, Adad-nirari I's sickle-sword, the Alaca Höyük iron dagger, the Nuzi scale armour, the Nippur medical tablet). The **numbers** attached to them are invented.
